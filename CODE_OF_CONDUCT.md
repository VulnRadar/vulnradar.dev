# Code of Conduct

VulnRadar is an open-source security project, and it depends on people being
able to collaborate without harassment or hostility. This document sets the
behaviour we expect from everyone who takes part: contributors, maintainers,
issue reporters, and anyone in our community spaces (the repository, issues,
pull requests, discussions, and the Discord server).

## Our commitment

We want participation in VulnRadar to be a welcoming, respectful, and
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability,
ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate. Assume good faith and disagree without being
  disagreeable.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the community.
- Respect that this is a security project: report vulnerabilities responsibly
  through the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), never by dropping a
  working exploit in a public issue.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination in any form.
- Insulting, derogatory, or demeaning comments, and personal or political
  attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information (such as a physical or email
  address) without their explicit permission.
- Using VulnRadar, its issues, or its tooling to plan, coordinate, or brag
  about attacking systems you are not authorised to test.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or any other conduct that would
  reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces and also applies when an
individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the
maintainers at **support@vulnradar.dev**. Include what happened, where, and any
context that helps (links, screenshots). Reports are handled discreetly, and we
will not name a reporter without their consent.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.
They may take any action they deem appropriate, up to and including a permanent
ban from all project spaces, and may remove, edit, or reject contributions,
comments, commits, issues, and other interactions that violate it. Enforcement
decisions are made in good faith and in the interest of keeping the community
safe and productive.
